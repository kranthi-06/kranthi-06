## About Me

I'm **Kasa Kranthi Kiran** — an AI Systems Engineer specializing in **full-stack development**, **machine learning**, and **computer vision**. I architect and ship intelligent products that move from research prototypes to production-ready systems with clean interfaces and reliable infrastructure.

I build **AI-native applications** — goal-tracking platforms with predictive analytics, computer vision pipelines for agriculture and accessibility, IoT monitoring systems, and portfolio-grade web experiences. My work spans **React & Next.js frontends**, **Python ML services**, **Node.js & FastAPI backends**, and **cloud-deployed full-stack products**.

Currently pursuing **B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (AI & ML)** with deep focus on agentic AI, generative systems, and product UX for intelligent software. I've shipped live products, competed nationally in hackathons, and interned across AI engineering and full-stack roles — always optimizing for systems that **scale**, **learn**, and **stay useful**.

**Specializations:** AI Engineering · Agentic Systems · Computer Vision · Full-Stack Development · Cloud Architecture · Product Design for AI
